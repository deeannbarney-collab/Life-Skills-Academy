🔍 Problem Solving

🎯 The 5-Step Problem Solving Method

Work through any problem systematically:

Step 1: Define the Problem Clearly
Step 2: Brainstorm Solutions
Step 3: Evaluate Options

Rate each solution on:

  • Feasibility (Can I actually do this?)
  • Impact (Will it solve the problem?)
  • Resources (Time, money, effort needed)
Step 4: Choose & Act
Step 5: Review Results

After trying the solution, ask:

  • Did it work?
  • What would I do differently?
  • What did I learn?
💡 Problem Solving Tips:
  • Break big problems into smaller parts
  • Look for root causes, not just symptoms
  • Consider asking for help or advice
  • Sometimes "do nothing" is a valid option
  • Learn from failures - they're data points

🔧 The 4-Step Resolution Process

1. Cool Down - Take space if emotions are high
2. Listen First - Understand before being understood
3. Find Common Ground - What do we both want?
4. Create Solutions Together - Collaborate on the path forward
💡 Conflict Resolution Tips:
  • Focus on the issue, not the person
  • Stay curious, not defensive
  • Validate feelings even if you disagree
  • Take breaks if needed
  • Aim for win-win solutions
  • Know when to agree to disagree

🏥 First Aid & Safety

🚨 Emergency Response Priorities

Remember: D.R.A.B.C.

D - Danger: Check for hazards to yourself and others
R - Response: Check if person is conscious
A - Airway: Ensure airway is clear
B - Breathing: Check for breathing
C - Circulation: Check for pulse, control bleeding
